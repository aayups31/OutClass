Your learning stays yours.

OutClass, previously GitLearn, is a local Windows learning workspace with a Chrome extension for Udemy. Local features do not require an OutClass account. It does not run a hosted coding workspace, sell personal information, or collect product analytics.

Browser extension

On Udemy course pages, the extension reads course title, course URL, lecture titles, sections, the current lesson, and visible completion indicators. It sends that context only to your paired local OutClass companion at 127.0.0.1:47831. Pending course updates and the pairing token are stored in Chrome extension storage. The extension does not read passwords, general browser history, cookies, or your course-platform credentials.

When you choose Capture this lesson, the extension reads the active learning tab to suggest a title, link, and material. It uses the text you selected or the page’s visible learning content, including an open transcript. You can edit the preview and opt in to including visible lesson links. Save sends the preview to your paired local companion. The Udemy transcript button also saves the visible transcript to the local library.

The extension's data disclosures cover local pairing authentication, saved lesson links and website text, and current lesson and completion context from Udemy course pages. Removing the extension removes its Chrome-local storage. Learning records saved to the companion remain on your computer.

Limited use

OutClass's use of information received through Chrome extension APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data supports the disclosed learning and pairing features only. It is not sold, used for advertising or lending, or transferred for unrelated purposes. An optional included-AI service, when configured, processes the context you explicitly send for learning help. Optional AI and GitHub processing in the companion is described below and controlled by the features you choose to use.

Local files and saved state

Course data, session notes, imported text, search indexes, embedding vectors, chat history, course project bindings, resume positions, and bounded local run evidence, practical task attempts and saved source snapshots, reflections, review schedules, project command records, and contribution briefs, idea notebooks, project decisions and Blueprint conversations are stored in OutClass’s SQLite database. Automatic notebook notes are opt-in and use saved reflections from explicitly linked courses. Your working code and reviewed checkpoints live in the learning folders you choose. You can remove imported sources in the app. Your course folders and app data are retained when you uninstall so your learning is not discarded; you can delete them yourself after exporting anything you need.

AI connections and project ideas

AI is optional. Choose a personal OpenAI key, an explicitly configured local model, or included AI through a configured hosted service. Included AI has been verified in an owner-only preview. Public sign-in and live billing are not yet available. The desktop app stores your personal OpenAI key and included-AI account session using Windows encryption, outside course folders. The source-development companion can read its own local .env instead. Keys are never shipped in downloads, sent to GitHub, or passed to the extension.

When enabled, OffCourse can send your question, selected code, relevant local code or notes, and bounded chat context to your selected AI connection. Course mode sends your question and relevant imported course passages. When you explicitly attach a recorded run or choose error help, its saved entry-file source and bounded output are included. During a practical task, Code help also includes its brief, checks, reflection, and recorded help use. During contribution work it includes the selected public issue, contributor instructions, your notes, and bounded recorded command results. Searching the Learning Library runs locally. When you ask OffCourse from the library, it sends your question and relevant passages from the courses you select. Drafting a practical sends an excerpt of the selected saved lesson to your AI connection. The Blueprint sends the selected project's notes and linked course goals for requested conversations or reviews. Sharing a notebook with OffCourse is optional and requires selecting it in a question. Original thoughts remain separate from AI interpretations. Editing or deleting a note clears derived local project history; previously transmitted context cannot be recalled from a provider. Semantic indexing in personal-key mode sends uncached source passages for embeddings when you explicitly refresh the index; question retrieval may send the question for an embedding. AI commit messages use a small scanned diff when separately enabled. The app scans sources for recognizable secrets before indexing or sending them. This does not replace reviewing your own content.

OutClass requests no response storage when using the OpenAI Responses API. OpenAI’s processing and retention are governed by its own terms and the provider account settings. Personal-key charges are billed to your OpenAI account. Included AI uses the service's account and allowance; its service records account identity, request/usage counters and subscription identifiers, without storing prompt text. A local model receives context at the configured localhost endpoint. Local and included modes use local keyword retrieval instead of sending embeddings through a personal key. See OpenAI’s data controls.

GitHub and editor tools

The desktop terminal is a full local shell with your account's permissions. It can modify files, install packages and contact services through commands you run. OutClass excludes its own provider/account credentials from the shell environment. Shell sessions and their bounded display history stay in memory and stop when the application closes. Arbitrary terminal output is not automatically treated as verified learning evidence.

The optional VS Code / Code OSS companion uses a separately paired, course-scoped local credential held in the editor's SecretStorage. It sends saved selected code and explicit questions to the local companion. It cannot access local setup, notebook management or terminal APIs. Your editor and other extensions have their own permissions and privacy policies. You can revoke its pairing in OutClass.

GitHub is optional. OutClass uses Git and GitHub CLI to connect your account and a repository you choose. Reviewed code and notes are uploaded only when you configure a remote and sync. Repository access and uploaded contents are governed by your GitHub account and repository permissions. Live work makes read-only GitHub requests for public issue, repository, contributor-guide, and discussion data. These requests reveal your selected language or repository and ordinary connection information to GitHub. Choosing a brief creates a local record; cloning creates a separate local branch and folder. OutClass prepares a local pull request draft and does not publish comments, forks, or pull requests for you.

Public website and downloads

This static page includes no analytics, advertising, contact form, or connection to your local workspace. Fonts and images are served with the site. The hosting and download providers may process ordinary connection information such as IP addresses under their own policies. External resource links open the relevant provider’s website.

The account page sends your email, password when creating an account or signing in with a password, and verification code to OutClass’s hosted authentication service, Supabase. Passwords are handled by Supabase and are not saved in browser storage. Browser sign-in uses host-only Secure, HttpOnly cookies with SameSite protections; authentication tokens are not returned to page JavaScript. The access cookie follows the token’s expiry, and the refresh cookie is retained for up to 30 days or until you sign out. Account responses are not cached. Sign-out clears the browser cookies and attempts to revoke only that browser’s provider session. Desktop sign-in is separate.

When you turn on progress sync in OutClass Learn, the app saves course names, recent lesson titles, progress counts and verified skill labels to your account. Your code, notes and local folder paths stay on your device. You can turn off syncing from the Learn profile. Signed-in devices with sync enabled can read the saved summaries until they are replaced or the account is deleted.

If you complete your learning profile, your name, selected interests, learning goals, and industries are stored in your Supabase account metadata. You can edit these on the account page. These preferences do not grant AI access, change billing, or publish your profile.

The account page displays your allowance and can open Stripe Checkout or the customer portal. OutClass does not receive card details. The connected subscription is currently in Stripe test mode, and email delivery is limited to the connected test account. Public learner access and live payments are not enabled by this interface.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions, open an issue in the OutClass repository without including API keys, personal course transcripts, or private code. Changes to data handling will be reflected here before a release that introduces them.
